## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE wide horizontal sprite sheet for a CRAFT book-reader onboarding comic, with exactly three equal-width cells in a single row. The supplied images are STYLE AND CHARACTER IDENTITY REFERENCES only; do not reproduce their words or composition. Preserve the distinctive violet stick people exactly: round outlined heads, small asymmetrical angular faces with horizontal eyebrows, short straight nose, tiny calm mouth; thick rounded stick arms/legs and torso; slender adult proportions, understated intelligent expression. Single ink color #584193 on pure white background. Each cell contains one full-body character at the same size with generous clean white padding, feet at the same baseline, no cell borders. Cell 1: standing character turns slightly right, holds a small simple rectangular smartphone in left hand, right arm clearly points to the right. Cell 2: standing character raises right arm diagonally up to the right, pointing at an imaginary button; left hand on hip. Cell 3: character sits comfortably cross-legged reading a simple open book, quiet satisfied expression. Intended as real finished editorial line illustration, clean smooth consistent CRAFT strokes matching references, not generic icons. No gradients, shading, shadows, realistic anatomy, clothing, decoration, extra faces, speech bubbles, text, letters, numbers, or logos. Leave all UI labels and instructional text to HTML. Wide canvas about 3:1, three isolated poses each centered in its own equal-width third, entirely visible.
